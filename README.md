# My Green Kampung Challenge

> **Maturity:** Interactive community-ESG concept demonstration

A multilingual front-end concept for helping neighbourhoods discover environmental challenges, compare community activity and visualise a possible ESG participation programme.

**Live demonstration:** [my-green-kampung-challenge.netlify.app](https://my-green-kampung-challenge.netlify.app/)

## Business problem

Community sustainability programmes often spread information across posters, chat groups and one-off events. This concept explores a shared digital front door for challenges, community directories, recognition, participation pathways and high-level impact storytelling.

## Intended users

- Residents and families
- Youth and community leaders
- Schools, NGOs and potential CSR participants
- Local-authority programme teams evaluating a digital engagement model
- Portfolio reviewers assessing community-platform and ESG experience design

## Demonstrated capabilities

- Responsive multilingual experience in English, Bahasa Melayu, Chinese and Iban
- Programme overview and activity storytelling
- Challenge and toolkit-style discovery cards
- KJM community-directory presentation
- Community leaderboard and recognition interface
- Animated counters and ESG-impact visualisations
- Participation pathways for residents, youth, leaders, schools, NGOs and CSR teams
- Mobile navigation and scroll-triggered visual effects

## Strategic value

The prototype makes a community programme tangible enough for discussion and usability review. It shows how a future service could combine public education, recurring challenges, community recognition and structured ESG reporting in one accessible experience.

## What is actually implemented

This repository contains a single static HTML application with embedded CSS and JavaScript. Language switching, animation and counters run in the browser. Links labelled register, apply or contact are interface placeholders, and there is no database, account system, form submission, leaderboard engine, verification workflow or reporting backend.

Displayed communities, rankings, activities, dates, counters and impact figures are demonstration content unless separately verified against an authoritative programme dataset.

## Technology

- HTML5
- Embedded CSS and responsive layouts
- Vanilla JavaScript for language switching, animations and counters
- Static Netlify delivery
- No build system, backend, persistence, authentication or external API

## Responsible-use boundaries

- References to DBKU, KJM communities, schools, NGOs, corporate partners or KOBIS describe the prototype’s scenario and intended stakeholder landscape; they must not be read as verified affiliation, commissioning, endorsement or partnership without written evidence.
- Community counts, rankings, points, dates, certificates and ESG figures are not operational records.
- Environmental impact reporting would require defined methodologies, evidence collection, baseline data, verification and transparent calculation rules.
- Production registration would require consent, privacy notices, identity and role controls, moderation, security and data-retention policies.
- Recognition or rewards should use published, auditable criteria with an appeal process and human oversight.
- Third-party names, marks, images and programme materials require appropriate permissions before operational use.

## Current limitations

- Calls to register, apply and contact are placeholders
- No member, community or administrator accounts
- No persistent challenge submissions, evidence uploads or points calculation
- No verified live community directory or leaderboard feed
- No operational ESG measurement or downloadable reporting
- No automated tests or production monitoring are evidenced in this repository
